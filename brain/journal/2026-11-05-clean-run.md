## Two things worth knowing

- **My first tally from night.log said 16, and the export says 17.** 71b46b0 was
  stopped twice by the owner gate (DEGRADED, the machine was in use) and resolved on
  its third try. A first-row tally picks the degraded row. The export counts only the
  attempt that measured the agent, which is the rule.
- **The specimen card was blocked by a select key that filtered nothing.**
  `export_site._specimen` sits the card only beside a claim's plain rate
  (`arm`, `cond`, `repos`), and claims_write.py had given the clean claims
  `limit_s: 5400`. Every row in the artifact ran at 5400 s, so the key changed no
  number, but the export read it as a time-limit slice and held the card back. The
  two clean claims now select the whole run, and the limit stays in their config.
